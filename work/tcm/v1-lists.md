# TCM v1 — closing lists

## 1. Absent

- `identification.practitioner_estimate`: no current count of TCM practitioners in China, Australia or elsewhere, with source and year. The only headcount is the historical figure of about 1.5 million barefoot doctors, who were trained in both Western medicine and TCM, so it is not a TCM practitioner count.
- `canon_and_transmission.dating_and_authorship_disputes`: no named scholars or positions on the dating and compilation of the Huangdi Neijing, the Shanghan Lun/Jingui Yaolue (including Zhang Zhongjing's authorship), the Nanjing, or the Shennong Bencao Jing.
- `disruption.loss_of_patronage`: nothing on whether or how the end of imperial or dynastic patronage at the close of the Qing affected the institutions sustaining Chinese medicine before the Republican-era abolition attempt. No dates or institutions are given.
- `revival_and_institutionalization.internal_factions`: no named 20th-century factions (purist versus integrationist, over integration with Western medicine and standardization), and no leading figures or dates.
- `literature_state.corpus_scale_appraisal`: no evidence map, bibliometric analysis or overview of reviews that characterizes the whole TCM trial and review corpus with counts by modality and country.

## 4. Schema misfit

- **Market and economic scale.** Section 8 of the report gives domestic TCM industrial output (~900 billion RMB, 2024 industry estimate), Yunnan Baiyao Group's 2024 revenue (CNY 40.03 billion) and global market estimates (~USD 29 billion to above USD 280 billion), with the report's own caveat that the global figures are unreliable. No field holds industry or market size. Proposed field: `economics`, with leaves such as `domestic_market_size`, `global_market_estimates` and `major_manufacturers`, each carrying source type and year. Commercial research firms would be flagged indirect.
- **Framing issues handled inside existing leaves (not misfits; recorded in notes):**
  - field 8 `colonial_power` and `post_colonial_disruption`: the disruption was domestic modernization, not colonial rule.
  - field 9 `revival_context` and `dated_events`: Western diffusion after 1971 is not revival in the country of origin.
  - field 11 `biomedical_content_in_curriculum`: the report describes TCM content in biomedical education, the reverse direction.
  - field 1 `system_boundary`: the relation from premodern traditions to modern TCM is closer to reconstruction or descent than to any relation value; `absorbed_strand` was used.
