# TCM v2 — closing lists

## Absent

- `diagnostics.sign_vs_synthesis`: inter-rater reliability for individual TCM signs (tongue color/coating/shape, pulse qualities) compared with agreement on the integrated pattern. The report gives pattern-level and point-selection agreement only.
- `disruption.colonial_power`: Q4 is headed "Colonialism and modernization" but names no colonial power. Missing: whether and where a colonial or foreign power governed Chinese medical practice (e.g. Hong Kong, Taiwan), and the report's position on the colonial framing for China.
- `disruption.post_colonial_disruption`: whether the 1949 PRC/Taiwan division, or Hong Kong's separate status, split or relocated Chinese medicine institutions and practitioners.
- `literature_state.corpus_scale_appraisal`: no evidence map, bibliometric analysis or Cochrane overview of the whole body of TCM clinical research (total trial counts by modality, language and database, e.g. CNKI). Vickers 1998 covers only country-level positivity to 1995.
- `contemporary_controversies.intellectual_property`: TCM patent disputes, biopiracy cases, Chinese traditional-knowledge or defensive databases, and Nagoya Protocol exposure for TCM materials.

## Schema misfit

- The report has a research-methodology controversy (validity of sham acupuncture as placebo; trial design around patterns; opposing readings of the Vickers effect size) with no dedicated leaf. It is placed in `contemporary_controversies.integration_disputes` and cross-noted in `evidence_state.reviews` methodological limitations; that is an imperfect fit, noted in `notes`, not a misfit. Framing misfit: the `disruption` field, and specifically `colonial_power`, presumes a colonizer. The report frames China's rupture as dynastic decline, pressure from Western biomedicine, the Meiji Japanese model and domestic scientism, so `colonial_power` came out `absent` (unclear) rather than `not_applicable`. Proposed clarification: allow `disruption.colonial_power` to record "semi-colonial / indirect foreign pressure" as a distinct value, so traditions displaced under non-colonial modernization are not coded as gaps.
