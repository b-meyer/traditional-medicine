## 1. Absent

- `canon_and_transmission.secondary_canon` - no post-medieval or South Asian Unani textbooks or commentary tradition on the Canon.
- `canon_and_transmission.dating_and_authorship_disputes` - no named scholarly positions on dating or attribution of core Unani texts.
- `canon_and_transmission.redaction_history` - no redaction, recension or manuscript-tradition history for the Canon or al-Hawi.
- `theoretical_primitives.nosology` - no Unani disease classification (humoral or temperament-based disease classes).
- `disruption.suppression_or_marginalization` - no dated colonial or post-colonial policies marginalizing Unani.
- `disruption.abolition_attempts` - no documented proposals to defund or abolish Unani institutions.
- `disruption.practitioner_response` - no hakim associations, conferences or resistance bodies with dates.
- `disruption.post_colonial_disruption` - effect of 1947 partition and state succession on Unani colleges and practitioners not covered.
- `disruption.dated_events` - no dated disruption events (Mughal decline and the colonial period are undated in the report).
- `revival_and_institutionalization.nationalist_framing` - no sources on nationalist or communal framing of the Unani revival in early-20th-century India.
- `revival_and_institutionalization.internal_factions` - no named purist or integrationist factions within Unani in India.
- `literature_state.corpus_scale_appraisal` - no bibliometric analysis, evidence map or overview of the whole Unani clinical literature.
- `literature_state.geographic_concentration_of_trials` - no country distribution of Unani trials.
- `literature_state.registry_and_reporting_practice` - no data on trial registration (CTRI etc.) or CONSORT adherence in Unani trials.
- `safety.documented_adverse_events` - no Unani-specific case series or pharmacovigilance data on kushta or other preparations.
- `safety.regulatory_actions_taken` - no bans, alerts or advisories on Unani or kushta products in India, Pakistan or elsewhere.

Partial leaves with specific gaps noted in `notes` (not counted above) include `disruption.loss_of_patronage` (dates), `disruption.colonial_power` (colonial power unnamed), `legal_status.jurisdictions` (statute, regulator and tier for Pakistan, Iran and Bangladesh), `literature_state.publication_bias_findings` (no study documenting it), `safety.adulteration_and_contamination` (no measured Unani figures) and `revival_and_institutionalization.standardization_events` (Tibbia College and CCRUM dates).
