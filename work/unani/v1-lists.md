# Unani v1: closing lists

## 1. Absent

- `canon_and_transmission.redaction_history`: no manuscript or recension history for the Canon or for the Indo-Persian and Urdu Unani texts used in South Asia.
- `theoretical_primitives.nosology`: no Unani disease classification (categories of disease in the classical texts or in the modern Unani curriculum and terminologies).
- `disruption.colonial_power`: the colonial power and its administration are never named. The report says only "colonial biomedicine".
- `disruption.suppression_or_marginalization`: no dated colonial medical policies affecting Unani practitioners (registration acts, exclusion from state medical services, funding).
- `disruption.abolition_attempts`: no formal proposals, votes or official reports to abolish or restrict Unani, and so no dates or outcomes.
- `disruption.post_colonial_disruption`: no account of how the 1947 partition affected Unani institutions (the division of Hamdard, relocation of hakims and colleges), although both Hamdard entities are mentioned.
- `disruption.dated_events`: no dated disruption events. The Mughal decline and the loss of patronage are undated.
- `revival_and_institutionalization.nationalist_framing`: no treatment of nationalist or anti-colonial framing of the early-20th-century revival (e.g. Ajmal Khan's political role).
- `revival_and_institutionalization.internal_factions`: no purist-versus-integrationist or other named splits in the Unani revival in India or Pakistan.
- `literature_state.corpus_scale_appraisal`: no appraisal of the whole corpus (bibliometric analysis, evidence map or Cochrane overview of Unani clinical research). The one evidence map cited covers post-COVID-19 recovery only.
- `literature_state.geographic_concentration_of_trials`: no country distribution of Unani trials.
- `safety.documented_adverse_events`: no Unani-specific case reports, case series or pharmacovigilance data. Every adverse-event figure is Ayurvedic.
- `safety.regulatory_actions_taken`: no bans, import alerts, recalls or advisories on Unani products or kushta, with dates.

Partial leaves with specific missing items (named in each leaf's `notes`):
- `canon_and_transmission.foundational_texts`: no specific Hippocratic or Galenic titles, and no modern curricular canon.
- `canon_and_transmission.dating_and_authorship_disputes`: no disputes over the dating or authorship of any text.
- `canon_and_transmission.transmission_mode`: little on lineage or apprenticeship teaching.
- `canon_and_transmission.external_dating_anchors`: no dated manuscripts.
- `theoretical_primitives.translation_programs`: no named studies.
- `disruption.loss_of_patronage`: undated.
- `disruption.period`: undated.
- `disruption.practitioner_response`: no bodies or associations named, no college founding date.
- `revival_and_institutionalization.revival_context`: the report gives the pressures (lost patronage, colonial biomedicine) but does not say whether the revival was nationalist, state-led or market-led.
- `revival_and_institutionalization.standardization_events`: no textbook or curriculum dates, no CCRUM or Tibbia College founding dates, and no year for the 55-colleges figure.
- `legal_status.jurisdictions`: no scope of practice, prescribing rights, integration or recognition tier for India. No regulator or statute for Pakistan, Iran or Bangladesh.
- `literature_state.trial_counts`: counts only for vitiligo and post-COVID-19. No totals by modality.
- `literature_state.publication_bias_findings`: the report asserts bias but cites no study documenting it.
- `literature_state.registry_and_reporting_practice`: no registry data.
- `evidence_state.reviews`: no reviewer certainty ratings, no conclusion given for the post-COVID review, hijama reviews unnamed, no reviews of pharmacotherapy by condition.
- `evidence_state.preclinical_and_constituent_evidence`: no animal or in-vitro studies.
- `safety.adulteration_and_contamination`: no measured figures for Unani products.
- `safety.intrinsic_toxicity`: toxicity mechanism of high-degree drugs not given.

## 3. Unclear or indirect sourcing

The report has only seven [Sn] markers. Most claims name a source in the text (Britannica, Gutas, named journals) but have no bibliography entry, so they are recorded as `type: unclear`.

Indirect:
- `identification.practitioner_estimate`: 47,683 registered Unani practitioners in India (2015). Reached through a Business Standard/PTI news report of a Rajya Sabha reply.
- `revival_and_institutionalization.standardization_events`: 55 Unani colleges. Reached through a Medical Dialogues news report of Lok Sabha data; year not given.
- `canon_and_transmission.external_dating_anchors` and `canon_and_transmission.dated_events`: Ibn al-Nafis rediscovered in 1924. Source is S3, Wikipedia ("Commentary on Anatomy in Avicenna's Canon").
- `diagnostics.methods`: nabz, baul and baraz as the diagnostic tools. Source is S4, hakeemchichi.in, a Unani and Ayurvedic pharmacy website.
- `revival_and_institutionalization.standardization_events`, `revival_and_institutionalization.dated_events`, `legal_status.jurisdictions`, `education_and_licensure.accrediting_body`: CCIM replaced by the NCISM (NCISM Act 2020). Source is S5, IAS Gyan, an exam-preparation editorial site.

Mismatch (`claim_match: mismatch`):
- `revival_and_institutionalization.dated_events` and `contemporary_controversies.who_engagement`: WHO recognized Unani in 1976. Source is S6, Britannica "Unani medicine - Herbal Remedies, Diet, Exercise" (URL /Modes-of-treatment). The title covers treatment modes, not WHO recognition. It may be another section of the same article.
