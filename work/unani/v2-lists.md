- `disruption.loss_of_patronage`: dates and circumstances of the decline of Mughal court and Nizam of Hyderabad patronage of hakims and tibbi institutions, and its effect on Unani before and under British rule. The report mentions the patronage but not its decline.
- `disruption.abolition_attempts`: any colonial-era or provincial legislative proposals to prohibit or deregister Unani practitioners in British India (medical registration acts and how they treated hakims), with dates and outcomes.

The only corpus-type sources are scoped elsewhere (a general cupping evidence map, S19; a post-COVID Unani evidence mapping, S18). - `theoretical_primitives.nosology`: Unani disease classification beyond simple vs. material dystemperament.

- `disruption.post_colonial_disruption`: effects of the 1947 Partition beyond Hamdard, and of the 1971 separation of Bangladesh.
- `legal_status.jurisdictions`: scope of practice and prescribing rights in India, Pakistan and South Africa; statutes and regulators for Bangladesh and Sri Lanka; the legal instrument for Iranian TPM practice.
- `literature_state.trial_counts`: total number of Unani trials, overall and by modality.
- `literature_state.publication_bias_findings`: a specific study documenting publication bias in the Unani literature.
- `literature_state.registry_and_reporting_practice`: CTRI/IRCT registration rates and CONSORT adherence.
- `evidence_state.reviews`: a systematic review of Unani herbal formulations for a named indication, with GRADE certainty; GRADE ratings for the existing reviews.
- `evidence_state.preclinical_and_constituent_evidence`: named isolated constituents; animal and in-vitro studies of specific formulations.
- `contemporary_controversies.who_engagement`: Unani in WHO Traditional Medicine Strategy documents and ICD-11; WHO collaborating centres.
- `contemporary_controversies.politicized_episodes`: specific Unani COVID-19 products or advisories, with dates, and any regulatory or court responses.

## 4. Schema misfit

- **Fields whose framing does not fit well:**
  - `contact_and_borrowing.relationships` has no `kind` for pure convergence, where the report finds no contact. Chinese and Tibetan medicine were entered as `kind: exchange` with `status: convergence`, which reads as a contradiction. Proposed: add `kind: parallel` (no demonstrated contact).
  - `identification.system_boundary` relation enum (same_system_variant | sibling | regulatory_grouping) has no value for a distinct strand partly absorbed into the system (Tibb al-Nabawi, per Attewell/Alavi). It was entered as `sibling` with a note. Proposed: add `absorbed_strand` or `partial_incorporation`.
  - `safety.intrinsic_toxicity` mechanism classes (heavy metal | plant toxin | procedural | interaction) have no slot for delayed biomedical care or for undeclared pharmaceutical adulterants (steroids, antibiotics). Delayed care was recorded with a note. Proposed: add `systemic/indirect` and `pharmaceutical adulterant` classes.

Everything else fits an existing leaf, with mismatches noted in `notes`.
